# WordDeck Tatoeba SentencePack provenance notice

This notice applies to the Round-3 candidate SentencePack generated by `tools/worddeck/build_tatoeba_cc0_pairs.py` and the WordDeck Tatoeba SentencePack builder.

## Sentence text selection

The generator downloads the official Tatoeba per-language English and Ukrainian `sentences_CC0` exports and the official English-Ukrainian links export from `https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/`.

A pair is eligible only when:

1. the English sentence ID is actually present in the official English CC0 sentence export;
2. the Ukrainian sentence ID is actually present in the official Ukrainian CC0 sentence export; and
3. the two IDs occur as a linked EN-UA pair in the official Tatoeba language-pair links export.

The generated pair manifest records the upstream URLs, SHA-256 hashes and byte sizes of the downloaded compressed exports, the SHA-256 of the generated pair TSV, row counts and the exact selection rule. The WordDeck builder independently verifies the adjacent pair-manifest SHA-256 before it accepts the input as a redistributable CC0-text candidate.

## Licensing scope

The selected English and Ukrainian sentence texts are taken only from Tatoeba's official CC0 sentence exports and are identified by the generated pack as `CC0 1.0` text data.

The EN-UA relationship is derived from Tatoeba's language-pair links export. Tatoeba's downloads page describes the general downloadable data files under CC BY 2.0 FR while separately making part of the sentence data available under CC0. For that reason WordDeck preserves this Tatoeba source notice and provenance evidence for the link relationship and does not claim that the entire upstream links dataset is CC0.

Independent release audit should review this notice together with the generated manifest and the actual release artifact before the SentencePack is included in a public WordDeck release.

## No audio redistribution

This SentencePack pipeline uses sentence text and relation identifiers only. It does not download or redistribute Tatoeba audio.
