# Bundled Content — Long-Term Product Rule

The Version 2 starter corpus is a minimum, not a one-time QA fixture. Accessible Chess should maintain and grow a curated lawful built-in accessible learning collection across future releases.

Future product work may add additional public-domain/open-license/project-authored books, annotated games, exercises, courses, opening/endgame material and teaching packs. Content must stay versioned, provenance-aware, redistributable, accessible and consumable through the canonical Books/Training/Library models rather than through a parallel ad-hoc viewer.

Users may import their own lawful material, but importing external content must not be a prerequisite for first-use functionality or basic acceptance testing of shipped content surfaces.
