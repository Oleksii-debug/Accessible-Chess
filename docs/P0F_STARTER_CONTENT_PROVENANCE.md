# P0-F: provenance, curation and redistribution terms for offline starter content

The release W2 starter bundle is built ahead of packaging and is fully offline at
runtime. `AccessibleChess.exe` does not download a chess corpus.

## Lawful real-game starter/sample corpus

The release builder `tools/p0f_lawful_starter_bundle.py` reuses the already
qualified D07/#572 source primitive and pins one exact public source:

- source: Lichess standard rated database, January 2013;
- archive: `lichess_db_standard_rated_2013-01.pgn.zst`;
- source URL: `https://database.lichess.org/standard/lichess_db_standard_rated_2013-01.pgn.zst`;
- compressed SHA-256: `aa40b3671fa3cf1072eb182892cd90b0e1e003a4a5943492f64b77e7f3fd1635`;
- published source size: 121,332 games;
- license identity: `CC0-1.0`.

The compressed archive is build-time input only and is not shipped.

## Deterministic curation policy

Binding #177/#691 requires a genuinely curated instructional/sample set, not
merely the first N lawful records. The qualified policy is
`accessible-chess-p0f-real-sample-v1`.

Each candidate record is parsed by the canonical strict PGN boundary
`acs.pgn_roundtrip.parse_pgn_text(strict=True)` and is rejected unless all of
these are true:

- exactly one strict/recovery-free game is present;
- the Result tag and movetext result agree and are one of `1-0`, `0-1`,
  `1/2-1/2`; unfinished `*` records are rejected;
- Event, White and Black metadata are non-empty;
- the main line contains at least 20 plies;
- at least four opening plies are available for a deterministic opening-prefix
  stratum.

Selection is deterministic from source order, but representation floors are
mandatory rather than descriptive. A 240-game default release sample must
contain at least:

- 20 White wins, 20 Black wins and 8 draws;
- 20 games of 20-59 plies, 20 games of 60-99 plies and 8 games of 100+ plies;
- 12 distinct four-ply opening prefixes.

The builder scans at most 5,000 source records. It fails closed if the pinned
source cannot supply both the requested sample size and all representation
floors. Mandatory stratum representatives are selected first, then the remaining
slots are filled deterministically from the earliest eligible source records.

`manifest.json` records the policy identity, exact criteria, parser identity,
scanned/eligible/rejected counts, aggregate result/length/opening evidence and
one evidence record for every selected game: source index, Event, White, Black,
result, plies, length band, four-ply opening prefix and SHA-256 of that exact PGN
record. The bundle builder verifies that the ordered record hashes in the
curation evidence exactly match the bytes written to `starter_uk.pgn`.

This is machine-verifiable curation/representativeness evidence. It does not
claim that the source games contain project-authored commentary.

## Separate project-authored stress corpus

The deterministic 1200-game `stress_uk.pgn` remains a separate
project-authored load/search corpus generated by `acs/starter_content.py`. It is
used for import/search/paging qualification and is not counted as the >=200
curated real-game starter/sample set.

Project-owned stress-content license identity:
`LicenseRef-Accessible-Chess-Starter-Content-1.0`.

The stress material marked with this LicenseRef is created by the Accessible
Chess project. Permission is granted to use, copy, modify, and redistribute that
material with Accessible Chess or separately, provided this provenance and
license notice is preserved. This grant does not cover the Lichess source or
other third-party material.

## Final four-file payload contract

The W2 release builder materializes exactly these files under the caller-selected
release-content directory:

- `starter_uk.pgn` — pinned, strictly parsed, deterministically curated CC0 real-game sample;
- `stress_uk.pgn` — project-authored deterministic stress corpus;
- `sample_library.acsdb` — canonical ACSDB built from `starter_uk.pgn`;
- `manifest.json` — schema 3 release/provenance/license/curation evidence.

`manifest.json` also records source URL, exact compressed SHA-256, selected
subset SHA-256, game counts, `runtime_network_required=false`, and per-file
SHA-256/byte length/license identity. `starter_uk.pgn` and
`sample_library.acsdb` identify `CC0-1.0`; `stress_uk.pgn` identifies the
project-owned LicenseRef above.

W6/W5 must build this bundle into one fixed product-relative directory, preserve
these exact bytes through package preflight/assembly, fresh-extract the final ZIP,
and prove the application discovers/opens/searches the starter PGN/ACSDB offline.
Source qualification alone is not final package acceptance.

This lane does **not** claim physical NVDA acceptance. `HUMAN_ACCEPTED=NO` and
`NVDA_VERIFIED=NO` remain authoritative until Oleksii tests a fresh packaged
candidate.
