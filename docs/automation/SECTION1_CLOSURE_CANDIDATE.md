## 1.2 — FEN state fields and variant boundary

The Standard-chess surface covers side to move, orthodox castling rights,
en-passant, halfmove and fullmove fields.  Manual transitions clear stale
en-passant where required and validation remains owned by canonical
`Board` / `PositionState`.

Chess960/Fischer Random is not silently interpreted as Standard chess.  The
current product authority explicitly classifies that variant as unsupported
and fail-closed; Shredder-FEN/X-FEN rook-file castling evidence is not promoted
into the Standard-only rules core.  The plan says Chess960 only "where
planned", so this fail-closed boundary is the honest current Section 1
contract rather than a reason to invent a second rules implementation.

## 1.3 — SAN, moves, transitions and canonical legality

PR #2337 closes two residuals at the canonical Board boundary:

1. raw SAN / coordinate-move ingress shares one dependency-neutral
   `MAX_SAN_CHARS = 64` budget before normalization;
2. an explicitly supplied `+` or `#` must match the suffix generated by
   canonical `Board.san(move)`; omitted suffix remains accepted as historical
   human-input convenience.

The executable contract proves:

`legal Move -> Board.san -> notation grammar -> Board.parse_move -> Board.push
-> FEN -> undo/redo`

across castling, en-passant, promotion, source disambiguation and checkmate.
Wrong explicit check/mate claims fail without board mutation.

## 1.4 — positional interchange without a second rules authority

`PositionState` is the position-edit/interchange representation used by
Position Editor and related application surfaces.  It delegates playable
position validation to the canonical Standard `Board` boundary rather than
owning independent move legality.  EPD remains an adapter over this position
authority and does not become a second chess core.

## 1.5 — lawful/open corpus and mass verification

The exact #2337 workflow binds and rebuilds the project's pinned lawful CC0
real-game sample, then qualifies at least 200 games and 5000 canonical
positions.  It requires real coverage for both sides to move, castling
present/cleared, en-passant, non-zero halfmove and fullmove > 1.

Each projected position must be exact-six-field FEN and round-trip identically
through both canonical `Board` and `PositionState`.  Provenance, builder,
qualifier and compressed source digest are pinned; synthetic fixtures are not
substituted for this real-corpus gate.
